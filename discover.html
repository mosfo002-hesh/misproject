Discover Duluth!

Why did I choose UMD?

Being from the east coast, UMD is very different from anything I was used to growing up, but my entire family is from Minnesota and every summer I would drive through Duluth on the way to the North Shore to hike. These trips were always very memorable and I was always in awe of the beauty Minnesota has to offer. During my college admissions process I really didn't know what I wanted college wise, but I knew I'd be happy nearly anywhere. After coming back to Minnesota for winter break I was overwhelmed by the happiness I felt being in Minnesota with my family and enjoying the winter weather, so this heavily swayed my decision in coming to Duluth.

What I like about Duluth and the surrounding area:

Besides my family being 2 hours away, I love that Duluth has everything I need, but it is not a huge city and it has all the nature that allows me to explore and be active. The surrounding area is some of the most beautiful nature I have ever seen in my life and I'm so grateful to live so close to it. I love that there is so much to do in the area for nearly every type of person and there is always something to do for what you're feeling at the moment. Additionally, Duluth is an all-season town where there's something to do in every season because spirit mountain opens up for skiing in the winter which I am very excited about.
