My Hobbies

Softball

I played softball for 9 years of my life starting at nine years old and ending my senior year of high school. I absolutely loved the sport because of the team aspect and all of the amazing relationships I formed over the years. It was definitely physically and mentally challenging, but it pushed me in my everyday activities and taught me to be a better student and person. I started out playing in a recreational league called the Northern Virginia Girls Softball Association (NVGSA) where I met some of my lifelong friends. In sixth grade I joined a travel association called Crushers Fastpitch based out of Manassas, VA where I greatly improved on my skills and gained exposure to a higher level of playing. From there I played on a few different teams, the Stingrays and Team Virginia Mizuno. During my junior year, I decided I did not want to continue playing on a travel team and decided to commit my time to playing high school ball. My high school team was very special to me because all of the girls were so tightly bonded and we all saw each other everyday so it really helped our team chemistry. We also won our district twice while I played there which is an amazing accomplishment. I am so grateful to this sport for all the memories and relationships it has given me and it will always hold a special place in my heart.

Hiking

I have always loved to hike and my family has always been a hiking family. Growing up, I took road trips to Minnesota to go see my family and we would often take trips up to the north shore to go hiking. We stayed around Lutsen and hiked some of the same trails each time (Carlton peak, Oberg, and Eagle Mountain), but each time the hike was just as amazing and the view was as spectacular. In 2016, we took a trip to Banff in the Canadian Rockies which completely changed my life. I was completely mesmerized by the beauty of nature and I became fascinated with the outdoors because of this trip. The hikes were definitely very strenuous, but the view was worth it every time. Since then, I have done quite a bit of hiking in many places. Some of my favorites are: Shenandoah National Park, Panama, Alaska, Mallorca, Vermont, and Arizona.

Rock Climbing

Since coming to UMD, I have gotten into rock climbing because of my friends and it has turned out to be one of my favorite hobbies. I really enjoy the sport because it challenges me in ways that I have never experienced before and it shows me that I am capable of doing things I never thought possible. I really enjoy bouldering, which involves climbing on the holds on the wall without a rope, so it is not as high up. However, I very much enjoy top roping as well because it incorporates endurance which is very interesting to experience and push yourself to finish the route. With top roping, it is very fun to climb on actual rock outside in some very beautiful places. I recently went to Ely's Peak and climbed one of the slabs there which was a very fun experience. One other thing I love about climbing is the community. Everyone is there to have a good time and help each other out, so it makes it very encouraging to keep going on a climb even when you are very tired. I am very excited to see where this hobby takes me.

My first time climbing real rock at Ely's Peak!

Traveling

Traveling is a hobby that makes me feel so alive. I love being able to experience new places, cultures, and opportunities. As a Spanish speaker, I am always interested in traveling to Spanish-speaking countries so I can experience first-hand how Spanish speakers live their lives in different parts of the world and how the language changes in different areas. However, I am interested in traveling everywhere because there is a whole world out there to explore! I was fortunate enough that my parents also held value in traveling, so I went many places as a child, especially during roadtrips. I have been to 38 out of 50 states as well as 7 different countries. Some of my favorite places I have been to include: Panama, Banff, Alaska, and Arizona. I love Panama because there is such a rich mix of cultures with the indigenous population as well as the tie-in of Caribbean culture which also causes the language to have a funky dialect which I think is pretty cool. Besides that, it is also a gorgeous country that borders both the pacific ocean and the Caribbean with mountains, valleys, rainforests, and the Panama Canal. Banff and Alaska I both love because of their unique displays of nature that are not available anywhere else in the world. They both have unique colored bright-blue or bright-green colored waters that are absolutely stunning. Arizona is one of the most unique and beautiful places I have ever been and I find it very interesting because I have not spent much time in places that have deserts. It is also a place with some of the most beautiful sunsets. I cannot wait to continue exploring so many different places!
